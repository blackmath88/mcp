Where does a tool reach?

The same nine slots, laid out as a place. Pick a server and watch which slots it lights, what it reaches outside, and how a call travels. Tap a slot or a neighbour to see its tools.

C+ · Entrance, workshop, neighbours. You and the AI app stay outside. MCP is the front desk: the board is the tools/list, all the AI is shown, and everything crossing the desk is listed under it (roots in, questions out). Behind it is the server's workshop. Outside its walls are what the tools reach, named per server. Amber marks a rule in a wall opening, or something coming back out to you through the desk.
A · In, work, out. A call enters from the AI, takes something in, works on it, leaves a mark, and returns. Top row touches records, bottom row touches people and the world. The green line is the route this server's tools can take.
B · How far it reaches. The server sits in the middle. Rings are distance: inside the server, your records, then beyond the computer (you, another mind, the physical world). Further out means more consequence.
C · The workshop. One room, things where you would expect them: the shelf of records, the workbench, the window to you, the phone to another mind, the loading dock to the world.
